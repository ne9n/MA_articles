## Technical Summary of Key Revisions
1. **De-Fluffed & Shortened:** Removed unnecessary decorative prose, keeping the narrative fast-paced, direct, and authentic.
2. **Accurate Flight & Launch Operations:** 
   * Hand-launch is along the circular flight path (NOT into the wind).
   * Flight duration is 8 to 10 laps (approximately 90 seconds, NOT 2.5 minutes).
   * Instructor pilot guides the student on the handle throughout the flight using the dual-handle coaching technique (removed inaccurate claims of student flying solo).
   * Briefing simplified strictly to elevator operation (wrist tilt) and maintaining arm extension / line tension (removed visual reference).
   * Terminology standardized to "instructor pilots" (NOT flight coaches).
3. **Streamlined Names & Expanded Help Wanted Section:** Removed non-essential name lists while maintaining an actionable, structured call for instructor pilots, pit crew technicians, registration crew, and remote airframe builders/donors.
4. **Airframe Evolution:** Complete coverage of all five distinct historical eras (Personal Balsa $\rightarrow$ Cox RTF Plastics $\rightarrow$ Bratco Skybabies with Norvel .061s $\rightarrow$ Coroplast Tuff Baby $\rightarrow$ Electric Eagle 1).
