### Story Overview
Every summer at EAA AirVenture in Oshkosh, Wisconsin, Pioneer Airport introduces 1,400 to 1,800 youth per week to hands-on control line flying. Over the past three decades, this program has safely logged more than 25,000 introductory flights. 

This article traces the heritage of the venue—from Sean Elliott recruiting St. Louis modelers by landing EAA's full-scale *Spirit of St. Louis* replica at the SIG contest in Montezuma, Iowa, to the 30-year technical evolution of the training fleet (from personal balsa and Cox .049 plastic RTFs to Bob Arata’s Coroplast "Tuff Baby" and the modern brushless electric "Eagle 1"). It highlights the life-changing impact on youth who now fly as commercial airline captains and military pilots, and offers a clear invitation for the aeromodeling community to get involved on the flight line.
